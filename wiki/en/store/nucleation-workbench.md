# Nucleus Workbench

The Nucleus Workbench serves coordination designers and organizers. It provides Zhixu drafts, compile previews, supplier directories, resource configuration, publication material, proof, and audit records.

| Participant | Identity and rights | Source of rights |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Nucleus | Design Zhixu, organize candidate suppliers, maintain coordination material. | Nucleus governance and Store workspace access. |
| Store operator/reviewer | Maintain the catalog, review material, set visibility and recommendations. | Store institutional rules and access control. |
| Publisher | Sign Plan publication. | StateMachine publisher authority and EIP-712 signature. |
| Registry operator | Verify an offline subject and register its subject/account mapping. | Identity Registry owner authority. |
| Order participant | Accept an Order role and submit authorized Signals. | Order authorization, active executor overlay, and signature. |

Store capability tags, ranking features, and match history are off-chain commercial records. The Identity Registry provides public identity resolution. StateMachine records Plan, Order, Signal, HookReady, patch, and docking facts.
